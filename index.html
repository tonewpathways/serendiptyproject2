Welcome to Serendipity

Serendipity Sober Living is a warm, family-centered home for people building a new life in recovery. We know that lasting change happens best when people feel supported, respected, and truly at home — and that's exactly what we offer here.

Our residents are never just clients. They are family, and we walk alongside them with compassion, patience, and encouragement every step of the way.

Why Families Choose Us

At Serendipity, we believe that recovery is a journey best traveled with support, understanding, and a sense of belonging.

  • Compassionate SupportOur staff and housemates offer patience and encouragement, never judgment.
  • A True Home EnvironmentShared meals, tidy common spaces, and a household feel rather than an institution.
  • Real AccountabilityClear guidelines and daily structure give residents a steady foundation.
