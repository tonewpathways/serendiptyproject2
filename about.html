About Serendipity

At Serendipity, we believe recovery is not an accident — it's a choice made new every day. We provide a home where healing is honored, integrity is the foundation of every relationship, and hard work and determination are celebrated as the building blocks of lasting change.

Here, our residents are never just clients — they are family, and we walk beside them with compassion, accountability, and an unwavering belief in their ability to reclaim their lives.

Our Values

  • IntegrityHonesty is the foundation of every relationship in our home.
  • CompassionWe meet every resident exactly where they are, without judgment.
  • AccountabilityStructure and follow-through are how trust is rebuilt.
  • GrowthRecovery is a ongoing journey of learning and self-improvement. By offering structured classes in partnership with the Drug Free Dickson Coalition, we equip our residents with the knowledge, life skills, and tools necessary to build a sustainable new chapter.
