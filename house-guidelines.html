House Guidelines

Structure isn't a punishment here — it's how we protect the peace of the home we're all building together.

  1. Sobriety FirstSerendipity is a substance-free home. Random testing supports everyone's accountability.
  2. Curfew & Check-InsResidents check in by curfew each evening so everyone in the house can rest easy.
  3. Shared ChoresEvery resident helps keep common spaces clean — a shared responsibility, not one person's job.
  4. Respectful CommunicationWe speak to one another with the same kindness we'd want shown to our own family.
  5. House MeetingsWe encourage house meetings keep everyone connected and informed. Attendance is expected.
  6. Visitors & GuestsGuests are welcome during approved hours and must be registered ahead of time.
  7. Work, School, or Program CommitmentResidents are expected to be actively working, studying, or in a recovery program.
  8. Respect for Shared PropertyPersonal and shared belongings are treated with care — this home belongs to all of us.

A note to families: these guidelines exist to protect the warmth and safety of our home. Every resident is walked through the full house handbook on move-in day, and staff are always available to answer questions.

Serendipity Sober Living — a home, not just a program.
